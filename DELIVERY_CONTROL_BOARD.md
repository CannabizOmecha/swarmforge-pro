## Measurement contract

Every workstream reports:
- Artifact: immutable file/repository/version identifier.
- Test: command, environment, result, timestamp.
- Quality: independent reviewer/evaluator and findings.
- Business: qualified leads, pilots, conversion, revenue collected, refunds, direct costs and contribution margin where applicable.
- Reliability: failures, recovery time, rollback status and unresolved risks.
- Decision: continue, revise, pause or retire, with evidence.

## Autonomy and safety boundary

Agents may research, draft, code, test, simulate, prepare submissions and assemble evidence within approved scopes. Human authorization remains mandatory for spending, publishing, customer outreach, contracts, account changes, production deployments, data disclosure and other irreversible or externally visible actions. Do not fabricate test results, customer demand, platform approval, revenue or publication status.
